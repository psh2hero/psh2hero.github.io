## Episode Notes

In this episode of **5-Minute English Talk**, Chris and Jane teach you how to **keep a conversation alive for 10 minutes** without constantly searching for new topics or complicated vocabulary.

The secret is simple: instead of always looking for a new topic, learn how to go deeper into the conversation you already have.

You'll learn an easy conversation method:

**Ask → React → Share → Connect**

Ask a follow-up question.
React naturally to the answer.
Share something about yourself.
Then connect that answer to another question or related topic.

### In This Episode, You'll Learn:
- How to keep an English conversation going
- How to avoid awkward silence
- How to ask natural follow-up questions
- How to listen for details you can ask about
- How to use open-ended questions
- How to avoid making a conversation feel like an interview
- How to react naturally to someone's answer
- How to share your own experiences
- How to move naturally from one topic to another
- How to use “Speaking of…” to change topics smoothly
- How one simple topic can create a much longer conversation
Remember, you don't need ten different topics to have a ten-minute conversation.

Listen for small details, ask follow-up questions, react naturally, share your own experiences, and let one topic lead naturally to another.
